## 🇬🇧 Why this choice
CrisisMesh is a peer-to-peer store-and-forward delay-tolerant mesh communication engine for blackouts and network outages:
the Apache-2.0 license facilitates open adoption and interoperability among humanitarian relief initiatives, field responders, community meshes, and researchers,
while ensuring original attribution (NOTICE), reciprocal patent grants, and copyright protections remain intact.
Contributions adhere to the **DCO** (`git commit -s`). Value-added hardware integrations, specialized field support, and turnkey appliance deployments remain deliverable by the author.
